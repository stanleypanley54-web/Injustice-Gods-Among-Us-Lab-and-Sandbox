MODEL TRANSPARENCY

Scoring rules

The grade is a weighted heuristic of five categories: offence, defence, control, synergy and sustain. Each is estimated from explicit tags in character and gear records; pairwise team interactions add a limited synergy boost.

Team power computes from calculated workbook stats or your manual overrides. We do not treat that heuristic as the official matchmaking implementation. Scores remain heuristic and are NOT win rates.

Gear scoring remains approximate: the catalogue does not yet express a full per-fusion effect engine. An evolved requirement is not fulfilled automatically simply because gear appears in a sandbox slot.

How this sandbox works

1. Select three unique fighters. Set Elite rank and Level 1–50 to calculate stats from researched base values; switch to Manual for real displayed values.

2. Equip up to three unique gear pieces per fighter. The interface allows testing future unlocked slots—it's a sandbox.

3. Change Offence / AI Defence / Survivor. Ratings will shift to reward different roles.

4. Save named builds. Export JSON to transfer your collection and builds. GitHub Pages hosting is static; accounts do not sync between devices.

5. Edit tags and descriptions in Data Studio as research improves. Scores are explainable heuristics, not a combat simulation or a claim of exact damage.
